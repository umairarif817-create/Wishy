A Letter, For You

My love,

I wanted a page that was just words — no stickers, no decoration, nothing between you and what I actually mean.Wishy you are the most perfect human being I have ever seen and not just because I love you but because I see a genuine, loving, caring and an honest person in you. Your views about life, your idea of perfect family, your sacrifices for your friends, your endless efforts to make your parents proud, you consoling your friends whenever someone is going through something, you taking care of each and every person around you, you finding beauty in mediocre things, the Romanticism in words, gestures, time and emotions.If someone asks me to describe you I would probably say SHE IS A DEFINITION OF WHAT A HUMAN SHOULD BE. I learned so many things from you, corrected so many mistakes of mine from you and I will continue to learn from you all my life, Inshallah. I feel so emotional and happy seeing you turn 20, stepping into your practical life, starting a new chapter.Its like a father seeing the first steps of his daughter that's what u mean to me, Wishy.

A prose for you:

ابھی اس طرف نہ نگاہ کر میں غزل کی پلکیں سنوار لوں مرا لفظ لفظ ہو آئینہ تجھے آئنے میں اتار لوں میں تمام دن کا تھکا ہوا تو تمام شب کا جگا ہوا ذرا ٹھہر جا اسی موڑ پر تیرے ساتھ شام گزار لوں اگر آسماں کی نمائشوں میں مجھے بھی اذن قیام ہو تو میں موتیوں کی دکان سے تری بالیاں ترے ہار لوں کہیں اور بانٹ دے شہرتیں کہیں اور بخش دے عزتیں مرے پاس ہے مرا آئینہ میں کبھی نہ گرد و غبار لوں کئی اجنبی تری راہ میں مرے پاس سے یوں گزر گئے جنہیں دیکھ کر یہ تڑپ ہوئی ترا نام لے کے پکار لوں

I pray you always stay the same, their will be forever radiance in your eyes, a warmth in your smile and a burning sensation in your chest that keeps your heart on blaze. I hope this new begining for you comes a lot of blessings.May you fulfill all your dreams, your unsaid prayers and at the end of the year, you could look back and say Thank God A Good Year.

— always, [Your Umair]
