chapter eleven

A Letter, For You

My love,

I wanted a page that was just words — no stickers, no decoration, nothing between you and what I actually mean.

Thank you for being patient with me, for choosing me on the ordinary days and not just the easy ones, and for making a very average life feel like something worth writing about.

I don't say this enough, so I'm saying it here, where it can't be rushed: I love you. All of you, on every kind of day.

— always, [Your Name]
