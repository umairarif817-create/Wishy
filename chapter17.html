My Favorite Version of Us

Not the highlight-reel moments — the quiet ones, the beautiful one.The version of us that never give ups on eachother no matter how bad the things are, how badly we have fought with eachother and even if one of us has given up the other does not.One bend downs, one lowers his ego, one tries to make things right.That version of us is the prettiest thing I have ever witnessed.

That version of us is the one I'd choose on repeat, forever.
