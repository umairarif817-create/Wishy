Our Inside Jokes

I love our inside jokes, the things only we can laugh about, the things only we find amusing and funny and we can share our khikhi on em.For example when u say you are going to do susu and we both laugh for others it might not be a big deal or when you say you find out it's Cut day like it's Friday mandatory shower but it holds other meaning for us. Hundreds such little things to recall, to think about and am in love with all of em that this thing is particular for us and this thing has been fostered through our bond, our closeness our comfort with each other .

It's such a small, strange kind of intimacy — a whole language built for exactly two people.
