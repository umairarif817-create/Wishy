How It All Began

It all started with a random text, a random follow request, a random tantrums between us.I never knew that on 5th July I will meet the love of my life How that day gonna shape my future, my rest of the years.

I still think about that first conversation. How easy it was. How I wasn't even trying to be memorable, and somehow I couldn't stop thinking about you afterward.It took me 6 days to fall in love with you, just two days to see you in my dream and after those 6 days the only thing my mind could remember was Wishy Wishy Wishy....You were the most beautiful accident of my life.

I met you when you were 18.I witnessed the two most beautiful life changing years of you and i really hope I will be able to see the many more you are gonna have.To the new beginnings, for you, for me, for USS.
